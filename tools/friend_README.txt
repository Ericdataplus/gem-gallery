GEM GALLERY
===========

A walkable museum of real-world gems, minerals, crowns and treasures.

HOW TO START
  Unzip the whole folder anywhere, then double-click GemGallery.exe.
  Keep GemGallery.exe and GemGallery.pck together in the same folder.

  The first launch can take up to a minute while your graphics card prepares
  its shaders. Later launches are much faster.

  If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then
  "Run anyway". The game isn't signed, so Windows warns about it.

CONTROLS
  Click        look around (captures the mouse)
  W A S D      walk
  Shift        run
  Space        jump
  E            pick up the gem you're looking at; move the mouse to turn it,
               scroll to bring it closer, E again to put it back
  F            show or hide the labels
  Esc / Tab    free the mouse
  Q            quit

WHAT'S INSIDE
  You start among the raw mineral specimens. Walk forward (away from the
  start) to reach the cut gems and the treasure heap, the collector displays,
  the Legendary Gems hall, Ancient Egypt and the Maya, the Royal Treasury and
  the Collector's Wing. Far halls load in as you walk toward them.

NEEDS
  Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a graphics card with Vulkan support
  (most NVIDIA, AMD and Intel cards from about 2016 onward).
  About 1.5 GB of free memory.
